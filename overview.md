## 2. Global Canvas Coordinates & Layout Rules

- **Slide Dimensions**: Exactly `1080px` width × `1350px` height (Instagram 4:5 portrait aspect ratio). Constants defined in [`app.js:37`](file:///q:/.WORKSPACE/.CREATIVECONSULTING/instagram/automation/CarouselCreator/app.js#L37).
- **Panoramic Strip**: Slides are rendered continuously side-by-side (`x = slideIndex * 1080 + localX`). Handled in [`app.js:1981-2008`](file:///q:/.WORKSPACE/.CREATIVECONSULTING/instagram/automation/CarouselCreator/app.js#L1981-L2008) and [`app.js:2010-2108`](file:///q:/.WORKSPACE/.CREATIVECONSULTING/instagram/automation/CarouselCreator/app.js#L2010-L2108).
- **Seam Gaps**: When "Seam Gaps" toggle is active, a `28px` gap is inserted between slides to check cross-slide transitions. State managed in [`app.js:351`](file:///q:/.WORKSPACE/.CREATIVECONSULTING/instagram/automation/CarouselCreator/app.js#L351), applied in [`app.js:2044-2048`](file:///q:/.WORKSPACE/.CREATIVECONSULTING/instagram/automation/CarouselCreator/app.js#L2044-L2048) and [`app.js:3092-3096`](file:///q:/.WORKSPACE/.CREATIVECONSULTING/instagram/automation/CarouselCreator/app.js#L3092-L3096).
- **Layers**:
  - `Layer 1 (Bottom)`: Base Slide Background (textures, blueprint grid, SVG border frames) rendered via `buildSlideBackgroundHTML` in [`app.js:1638-1701`](file:///q:/.WORKSPACE/.CREATIVECONSULTING/instagram/automation/CarouselCreator/app.js#L1638-L1701).
  - `Layer 2 (Middle)`: Unified Panoramic Scene (Background images first, foreground images and text on top) rendered in [`app.js:1981-2008`](file:///q:/.WORKSPACE/.CREATIVECONSULTING/instagram/automation/CarouselCreator/app.js#L1981-L2008) and exported in [`app.js:3757-3766`](file:///q:/.WORKSPACE/.CREATIVECONSULTING/instagram/automation/CarouselCreator/app.js#L3757-L3766).
